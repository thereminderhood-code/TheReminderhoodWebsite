About Us

A neighbourhood of reminders: Muslims helping Muslims reconnect with Allah, one small bit at a time.

Who we are

Small bits. Lasting impact.

The ReminderHood is an Islamic education brand built by a small team of reciters, teachers and creators. We teach the deen in small, digestible bits, because a little knowledge that is understood and lived is worth more than a lot that is only heard.

Our work blends the Qur'an, authentic Sunnah and the classical scholars with careful teaching and cinematic storytelling, so that a reminder reaches the heart, not just the ears.

What we make

Reminders in many forms

  • Cinematic recitationsQur'an recited beautifully, with tafsir insights and reflection.
  • Deen in BitsShort lessons with a reference, a reflection and an action.
  • Reflection toolsGuides and journals for tadabbur and memorisation.
  • AppsCave Ash'ari and AjrHunter, simple tools for reciters.

Core values

What we hold ourselves to

IMAMAE

I

IkhlasSincerity

Ensuring that whatever we do is done essentially for the sake of Allah, for sincerity.

M

Mutaba'aFollowing the Sunnah

Following the example of RasuluLlah (May Allah's Peace Be Upon Him) in how he did things. In Islam, the 2 conditions for the acceptance of ibadah are Ikhlas & Mutaba'a.

A

AscensionExcellence

This entails having bias for action. It is the aspiration and decisiveness to seek excellence. It means we shouldn't sit with mediocrity and always look forward to being better.

A

AmanahTrust

This means taking every delegated task and responsibility as a trust and ensuring that we see them through to the end.

M

MuhasabahAccountability

The practice of taking one's self to account before we're taken into account. It is a deen version of accountability.

E

EmbodimentGrowth first

This entails having a growth first mentality, sharing knowledge primarily as a tool for self-purification and development. Others benefiting is only a byproduct and not the goal.
