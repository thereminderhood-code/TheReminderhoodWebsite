Recitation video studio

AjrHunter

Turn a Qur'an recitation into a video synced to the verses, so every listener can follow each ayah as it is recited.

Launch app (opens in a new tab) How it works

Why it exists

A recitation moves the heart most when the meaning travels with the sound. Yet many listeners hear the Arabic without being able to follow it, and the reminder passes them by.

Lining up every ayah by hand in a video editor takes hours, and a small slip in timing pulls the listener out of the moment. AjrHunter does the careful timing, so you can give your attention to the message.

How it works

From recitation to video

1

Upload your recitation

Add the audio, then choose the surah and the ayat it covers.

2

Synced to the verses

Auto sync times each ayah to the recitation. You can fine-tune any ayah's start and end.

3

Choose your fonts

Pick a Qur'anic script and a translation font, then set your colours, background and format.

4

Export the video

Download an MP4 with Uthmani Arabic above the translation, each ayah fading in and out on time.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it free?

Yes. You can create, sync and export verse-synced videos with AjrHunter for free.

Do I need an account?

No. You can make videos without one, and your edits stay in this browser. Create a free account if you want your edits saved to the cloud so you can reopen them on another device.

What file formats does it use?

Upload your recitation as MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, AAC or FLAC. Your finished video downloads as an MP4.

Is my audio stored?

Editing and video rendering happen in your browser. If you are not signed in, your audio stays on your device. If you sign in, your edits and audio are kept in private storage linked to your account, so only you can open them.
