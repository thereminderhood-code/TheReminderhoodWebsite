Recitation studio

Cave Ash'ari

Give your recitation the stillness of a vast, quiet hall: serene, spacious, and every letter still clear.

Launch app (opens in a new tab) How it works

Why it exists

Most recitations are recorded in small rooms, on a phone, and the sound stays small with them. The voice may be beautiful, yet it arrives flat and close, without the space that lets a listener settle into it.

Studio reverb is costly and easy to overdo; it can blur the very words it was meant to lift. Cave Ash'ari gives the recitation room to breathe, and keeps the letters clear.

How it works

Four calm steps

1

Record or upload

Recite straight into your browser, or upload a recording you already have. Trim the start, the end and any slips.

2

Processing

The voice is cleaned, levelled and gently compressed, so quiet and strong passages sit together naturally.

3

Preview the moods

Hear one-minute previews of each space (Subtle, Majestic, Cathedral and Hammam) and choose the one that fits.

4

Export

Master it and download a 320 kbps MP3 or a 24-bit WAV, ready to share or to edit into a video.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it free?

Yes. Recording, trimming, the four spaces and MP3/WAV export are free to use. A few extras inside the app are marked Premium.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use Cave Ash'ari straight away as a guest, and your work is kept in a library in your browser on this device.

What file formats does it use?

You can record in the browser or upload a common audio file such as MP3, WAV or M4A. Finished recitations download as a 320 kbps MP3 or a 24-bit WAV.

Is my audio stored?

Your audio is processed in your own browser and is not uploaded to us. Unfinished work and your library are saved on your device only, and you can delete them at any time.
